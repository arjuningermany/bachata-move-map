Bachata · practice tools

Learn the connections,
not the choreography.

Most classes hand you a new combo every week and you forget it by Sunday. These tools do the opposite — they show how a small set of moves fits together, so you can put them in any order on the floor.

The tools

Position map Open

Every position and what it opens up. Tap one to light up its moves and see where each leads. Filter by fundamental, intermediate, advanced.

Combo generator Next up

Builds a real sequence out of the map — pick a length and a level, get a fresh combo to drill. Every one is legal, because it follows the same connections.

Practice player Planned

Dance a generated combo in time. Counts the 8, marks the hip accents on 4 and 8, and cues the next move on the beat.

Progress tracker Planned

Mark each move as got it, learning, or not yet. The map colours itself in, so you can see what to work on next.
